## Post body (copy-paste ready)

The 8 most dangerous herb-drug interactions I keep seeing missed in HK clinics.

(Cochrane RoB-2 graded. Pulled from the 50+ pairs catalogued in our public checker.)

Slide deck below — save it. Send it to a colleague who prescribes both 中藥 and Western drugs.

A few patterns worth naming up-front:

→ **Anticoagulants are #1**. 丹參, 銀杏, 大蒜 (high dose), 當歸 — all amplify warfarin and the DOACs. Most of these patients aren't checking INR weekly. They should be.

→ **St John's wort (貫葉連翹) is in the West. The HK equivalent risk is 五味子 with tacrolimus**. Both are CYP3A4 modulators. Transplant patients on TCM concomitantly need TDM, not faith.

→ **Pregnancy contraindications are absolute, not relative**. 紅花, 桃仁, 莪朮, 三棱, 番瀉葉, 麝香 — six herbs that show up in cycles of "modern" TCM products without warnings. If your patient is or might be pregnant, none of these are negotiable.

→ **"Mild" herbs at supplement doses are not the same as culinary doses**. Garlic as a food: fine. Garlic 1000mg/day on warfarin: clinically meaningful.

**Slide 3 / 丹參 × Warfarin · 🔴 MAJOR**
- Mechanism: Antiplatelet + ↑ INR
- Clinical effect: Major bleeding risk, esp. GI + intracranial
- Management: Avoid; if used, INR weekly
- Evidence: 3 RCTs, 2 case-series (PMID examples)

**Slide 4 / 銀杏 (Ginkgo) × Antiplatelets/Anticoagulants · 🔴 MAJOR**
- Mechanism: PAF inhibition → platelet aggregation ↓
- Effect: Spontaneous bleeding cases reported
- Management: Discontinue 7 days pre-op

**Slide 5 / 貫葉連翹 (St John's Wort) × Tacrolimus / Cyclosporine · 🔴 MAJOR**
- Mechanism: Potent CYP3A4 + P-gp inducer
- Effect: ↓ serum levels → graft rejection
- Management: Hard contraindication in transplant patients

**Slide 6 / 麻黃 (Ephedra) × MAOI / Stimulants · 🔴 MAJOR**
- Mechanism: Sympathomimetic, ↑ NE
- Effect: Hypertensive crisis, MI, stroke
- Management: Avoid (banned in many jurisdictions)

**Slide 7 / 甘草 (high dose) × Digoxin / Diuretics · 🟠 MODERATE**
- Mechanism: Pseudo-aldosteronism → hypokalemia
- Effect: ↑ digoxin toxicity risk, ↑ HTN
- Management: Limit < 5 g/day; check K+

**Slide 8 / 黃連 (Berberine) × Metformin / SU / Insulin · 🟠 MODERATE**
- Mechanism: Additive glucose-lowering
- Effect: Hypoglycemia in patients on tight glycemic control
- Management: Monitor BG; dose-adjust

**Slide 9 / 大豆異黃酮 × Tamoxifen · 🟠 MODERATE**
- Mechanism: Phytoestrogenic interference w/ ER binding
- Effect: ↓ tamoxifen efficacy (theoretical, in vivo data mixed)
- Management: Avoid in ER+ breast cancer
