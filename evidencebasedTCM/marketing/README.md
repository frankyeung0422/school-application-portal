## How to use

1. **Each Monday morning**: open `week-N/mon-linkedin.md`, paste into LinkedIn, attach the image. Schedule for 8:30 HK time (9:30am HK = lunch time for Singapore + early morning for London).
2. **Each Thursday morning**: same with `thu-linkedin.md`.
3. **End of Week 2 and Week 4**: send the Substack newsletter.
4. **All images**: see `image-prompts.md` in each week folder. Use Canva (free tier is enough) for carousels; use Midjourney or DALL-E for illustrative images using the supplied prompts; OR take real platform screenshots where specified.

## Posting time conventions

- **LinkedIn**: 8:30am HKT Mon/Thu. Peak healthcare-professional engagement window.
- **Substack**: Sunday 9pm HKT (Mon 9am NYC). Reading time for the week ahead.

## Engagement rules (the part most people skip)

1. **Reply to every comment within 24h** — this is how trust forms.
2. **Comment on 3 posts/day** from MDs and TCM practitioners in your network. Substantive, not "great post!"
3. **DM 2 new practitioners/week** with a specific reason (e.g., "Saw your post on X — I'm building something on the same problem, would you give me 15 min of brutal feedback?").
4. **Don't auto-cross-post** to Twitter/X. LinkedIn-native posts perform better.
