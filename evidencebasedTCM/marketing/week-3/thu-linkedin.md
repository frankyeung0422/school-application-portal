## Post body (copy-paste ready)

Most TCM evidence is not Grade A. That's not a failure.

I catalogued 707 GRADE-rated studies on EvidenceBasedTCM.com. The breakdown:

- **Grade A**: 144 (20%) — strong recommendation, low risk of bias, consistent results
- **Grade B**: 308 (44%) — moderate confidence, methodologically reasonable
- **Grade C**: 184 (26%) — weak or inconsistent
- **Grade D**: 71 (10%) — very low confidence

Two things people get wrong about this:

**Mistake 1: "Only 20% Grade A means TCM doesn't work."**

Wrong. The Grade A bar is steep for every field. Statins have a higher Grade A density because there are tens of thousands of well-funded RCTs. TCM has hundreds, often industry-underfunded, sometimes methodologically older. Volume ≠ truth — but volume + funding does drive evidence-grade density.

**Mistake 2: "20% Grade A is enough — just prescribe based on those."**

Also wrong. Grade A in TCM is concentrated in a narrow set of conditions: COVID-19 (Lianhua Qingwen), post-stroke recovery (補陽還五湯), insomnia (酸棗仁湯), migraine (acupuncture, multiple SRs). The 80% of clinical conditions you'll see in practice live in Grade B/C territory. If you only prescribe what's Grade A, you stop treating most patients.

**The honest read**:

→ Grade A is a useful filter for **"is this likely to work?"**
→ Grade B is the working clinician's bread and butter — defensible, conditionally evidence-backed
→ Grade C/D is where you say "this is what's been done historically; the modern evidence is thin; here's why I'm doing it anyway" — and document the conversation

Five things in our database with Grade A evidence right now (with PMIDs in the comments):

1. **Acupuncture for chronic migraine prophylaxis** — Cochrane 2022, n=4,985 across 22 RCTs. Effect size superior to sham and comparable to oral preventives.

2. **黃連 / Berberine for T2DM glycemic control** — multiple meta-analyses, additive to metformin without serious AE signal.

3. **補陽還五湯 (Buyang Huanwu Tang) for post-stroke recovery** — multiple SRs, motor function improvement at 12 weeks.

4. **連花清瘟 (Lianhua Qingwen) for COVID-19 symptom duration** — 5+ meta-analyses, symptom resolution 1.5–2 days faster vs. placebo.

5. **逍遙散 (Xiao Yao San) for depression adjunct** — SRs showing additive effect to SSRIs, fewer GI side effects.

What's a TCM intervention you use that you wish had better evidence — and what would change if it suddenly had a Grade A meta-analysis behind it?
